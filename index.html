How to play

Mouse or keyboard. Use the +5 / +1 buttons (or type) to buy stock, type your selling prices, then press Open the stall or Enter to simulate the day. The forecast is right about 70% of the time. Hit ₹30,000 by the end of day 14 to win. Cash below ₹0 for a morning ends the game. Press R to restart.
